Privacy Policy

Information about how we collect and use your data

We at APVantage are committed to maintaining the privacy and security of the Personal Information of all visitors. At this web site and within our provided sites, APVantage collects information in three ways:

First, we passively collect information that is automatically sent by your web browser. This information typically includes your domain and user name. The amount of information sent depends on your browser settings. We use this information to develop statistics that are helpful to understanding how this site is used by our visitors.

Second, we actively obtain information about you by installing our own marker on your computer. This marker is commonly called a "cookie," and it enables us to know you by a computer-generated, unique identifier. By providing you a unique identifier, we administer site security and, in some cases, record previous choices and preferences. In situations where these choices or preferences need to be collected again, they can be supplied by us automatically, saving you time and effort.

Third, we collect information when you contact us or respond to the surveys, questionnaires or applications we make available. If you e-mail us or input information to the site, you are voluntarily releasing information to us. You may decide not to input information to the site; if that is what you choose, you may still continue your visit, but we may not be able to provide you the related services or features.

Any personal information collected on this site is provided voluntarily. We will not share your information with any third party outside of our organization.

By agreeing to the terms of our privacy policy you are consenting to our continued use of previously collected information. For your convenience, this page may contain certain hyperlinks to other APVantage or AP-Networks pages as well as to sites outside your company. While APVantage has posted an appropriate Information Privacy Statement throughout our web site, we can make no promises or guarantees regarding data collection on the hyperlinked pages and sites that are not owned by APVantage or AP-Networks. We therefore recommend that you read the privacy policy/statement for each site you visit.
